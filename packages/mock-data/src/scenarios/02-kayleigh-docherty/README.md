# Scenario 2: Kayleigh Docherty, 31, Ardvale, and her children Lily (7) and Mason (3)

## The story

Kayleigh lives at 8 Harbour Brae, Ardvale with Lily (P3 at Ardvale Primary, named person Claire Cowan) and Mason (health visitor Sunita Rao). Mason's father, Ryan Kerr (34), is Kayleigh's ex-partner. He lived with the family from early 2022 until his arrest in March 2025 for an assault that involved strangulation. He was convicted in November 2025 under section 1 of the Domestic Abuse (Scotland) Act 2018 and is on a community payback order supervised by justice social worker Helen Rae. He now lives at 15 Cannon Loan, Auchentorran.

The police lane shows six incidents since March 2024, each as serious or more serious than the last. Three of them came within weeks of Ryan regaining contact: staying over "for Mason" after the conviction, being let in to see Mason in April, and contact about the wee one over the summer. Kayleigh was first heard at Clydeshore MARAC on 8 January 2026 after a December incident with a DAQ of 15. Flags went on her GP and housing records the next day. The school was never flagged and learned of the January incident from Lily herself. A night of 34 calls and messages in June fell inside the 12 month repeat window but was dealt with as a single-agency warning.

On Saturday 22 August 2026 at 23:10 Ryan came to the house, shouted threats through the door and pushed Kayleigh into the hall when she opened it. Lily was on the stairs and saw it. Officers attended, a Child Concern Report went to the concern hub at 01:30, and DC Ewan Sutherland completed the DAQ the next morning: 17 yes answers of 27, high risk. Ryan was charged on 23 August and bailed on 24 August with conditions not to approach Kayleigh, the children or the address.

Two processes now run side by side and are linked both ways:

- **MARAC-2026-0093** (stage: research). Repeat referral from police received 24 August. Research requests went to eight agencies on 28 August with returns due today, 2 September. Police, health visiting, justice social work and children's social work have returned; the GP, housing and education requests are still open; Women's Aid returned nothing known. The MARAC is on 9 September, chaired by DS Paul Mackay and coordinated by Karen Findlay. Sadia Qureshi is the IDAA. Ryan has been referred to MATAC.
- **CP-2026-0431** (stage: investigation). IRD held 24 August at 11:00 for Lily and Mason. Both children judged at risk of significant harm, joint investigation opened, JII for Lily held 1 September under SCIM, no medical, no emergency order (bail conditions plus an interim safety plan), Reporter referral not made at this stage. The 28 day CPPM clock runs from the IRD and is due 21 September, 19 days from now. The CPPM is scheduled for that date with David Laird in the chair.

## What it proves

- MARAC and child protection running as two linked processes on one family, each with its own stage, clock and need-to-know matrix.
- The IDAA's role: Kayleigh does not attend the MARAC; her wishes are recorded, dated and represented.
- Research requests as sharing records: names and dates of birth only, one per agency, each with a lawful basis, visible in the recipient's worklist.
- Flags on health and housing records with a 12 month expiry, and the gap where education was never flagged.
- Perpetrator exclusion from need-to-know, enforced by rule rather than by remembering.
- The DAQ as a 27 question record with the score and band, distinct from the 24 question DASH.
- The integrated chronology showing escalation across agencies, with analysis kept apart from facts.
