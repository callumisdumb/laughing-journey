# Scenario 3: Derek Muir, 52, Auchentorran

## The story

Derek Muir was convicted in 2022 of sexual offences against a child and given an extended sentence of six years: a custodial term of four years and six months and an extension period of eighteen months. He is subject to Sex Offender Notification Requirements for an indefinite period. He completed the offending behaviour programme in custody, the Parole Board did not direct early release, and he was released from HMP Dunlarrick at the end of the custodial term on 22 July 2026, on licence until 21 January 2028.

The MAPPA record on the platform started ten weeks before release with a pre-release notification from the prison-based social worker. The sex offender liaison officer, Priya Sharif, referred him to Level 2 on 2 June, informed by an RM2000 (medium). The MAPPA Coordinator, Ross Mowat, sent pre-meeting requests to the single point of contact in each Responsible Authority and to housing. The pre-release Level 2 meeting on 14 July confirmed Level 2, agreed the Risk Management Plan, released Derek to staffed supported accommodation at 40 Abbey Wynd, Auchentorran, and set the review for 6 October: twelve weeks, the maximum interval for Level 2 under the MAPPA National Guidance 2022. The meeting also recorded that nothing goes to the victim through MAPPA; the Victim Notification Scheme is the route.

Six weeks on, he is compliant. He made his initial notification the day after release, has attended every supervision appointment with Helen Rae, has had four police home visits, and his Stable and Acute 2007 is stable moderate, acute low. Two things are live. Housing has proposed a settled tenancy at 6 Schoolhouse Loan, Craiglarrick, which is 180 metres from Ardvale Primary and faces a play park; Mark Hepburn's Environmental Risk Assessment started on 18 August and is not concluded. And Auchentorran Vehicle Recycling Ltd has offered him a job in a yard that is open to the public; a disclosure decision to the employer is pending with Priya Sharif, due 9 September, before the proposed start date. The review meeting has been called; police and justice social work have returned, health and housing have not yet, and SPS has nothing further to add.

The victim has no record on the platform and is referred to only as "the victim". The platform is not ViSOR: it holds the local MAPPA case record and the ViSOR reference as a string.

## Demo path

1. Sign in as Priya Sharif (Police Scotland, sex offender liaison). Home shows the Level 2 review clock at about five weeks and two actions due this month: the disclosure decision (9 Sep) and the ERA (12 Sep, owned by housing).
2. Open People, search "Muir". Open Person 360. The header shows the restricted classification, the MAPPA alert, the supported accommodation address with the pre-custody tenancy in the history, and SONR compliant with the next notification date. Note the ViSOR reference in the record panel: a string, not a link.
3. Open the MAPPA dashboard from the process card. Walk the five stages on the stepper: notification (SPS, 10 May), referral (2 Jun, with the three risk assessments listed by tool, date, assessor and band), pre-meeting returns, the 14 July meeting, managed. Open the level history and read the reason for Level 2.
4. Open the Risk Management Plan: triggers, contingencies, controls, victim safety, accommodation, employment, associates, plus the five licence conditions and the outcomes with their actions.
5. Open the Environmental Risk Assessment panel: proposed address, four concerns, assessor, no conclusion yet. Then the disclosure panel: recipient, the three facts that would be disclosed and nothing more, the rationale, status pending.
6. Open the Chronology tab. Every event is restricted. Click the ERA start event and the employment offer to see the audit and lawful basis in the drawer. Read Priya's analysis note on the accommodation risk in its own lane.
7. Open Meetings and the 6 October review: the pre-meeting requests show two returned, two requested and SPS nothing known. Open the 14 July minute: decisions with rationale, one dissent from housing about the ERA timing, the distribution list with housing on fields only.
8. Sign in as Mark Hepburn (housing). He sees the ERA conclusions and controls, the residence condition and the review date, and nothing else. Sign in as Rhona Dewar (inspector): redacted. Sign in as Janet Kerr (children and families social worker): no trace of the record, because there is no child in the household and no disclosure to children's social work has been made.

## What it proves

- The most restrictive process in the product: a restricted classification, every event restricted, a distribution list that is the need-to-know list, and an alert visible only to the Responsible Authorities.
- The five MAPPA stages and pre-meeting returns as first-class records, with the MAPPA Coordinator's role visible.
- Risk assessments recorded by tool, date, assessor and band, never computed by the platform.
- The Risk Management Plan and the Environmental Risk Assessment under the National Accommodation Strategy for Sex Offenders, with housing receiving fields only.
- Disclosure decisions to third parties as recorded decisions with facts and rationale, and the rule that MAPPA information is not given to victims or employers by any other route.
- The Level 2 review clock from the MAPPA National Guidance 2022: no less than once every twelve weeks.
